# Muqrakr
## Hi there 👋 Welcome to the Muqrakr suite of tools

### Follow the money in federal politics.
#### 💵 _“Cui bono?” — who benefits?_ 💵

Muqrakr puts campaign finance records and congressional voting records side by side, so you can see who funds your representatives and how they vote. Every figure comes straight from the Federal Election Commission, Voteview, and the Library of Congress. Currently, there are two versions of Muqrakr: the first being a full-stack web app that you can access [here](https://www.muqrakr.com/). The second version is a lightweight CLI tool that you download to your computer and do fast, simple searches of campaign finances.

Look up the campaign finances of candidates, campaign committees, and even voting records of members of Congress. Make the connections. What are you waiting for, get muckracking now!
